# SHINE AAC v0.4.1

Android version code: 61

## Camera-action setup

- Cheek movement setup now learns from unlabeled camera frames and finds useful
  movement samples automatically. A user no longer has to activate the switch six
  times before calibration can begin.
- Camera framing guidance keeps the whole face visible at roughly 70% of the preview
  instead of encouraging a face-filling crop.
- Camera setup uses plain **Camera action** and **Cheek movement** wording and no longer
  exposes internal Text-to-Speech engine or voice identifiers.

## MediaPipe face analysis

- Long blink and cheek movement now share one bundled, on-device MediaPipe face
  analyzer. Camera frames remain on the device and first use works offline.
- Long-blink calibration uses per-eye normalization and rejects weak or unsafe holds.
- Runtime blink decisions use bounded statistical evidence so an occasional uncertain
  or delayed frame does not interrupt a deliberate hold, while natural and short
  blinks remain rejected.
- Camera analysis is cadence-limited and stale holds are cancelled after a real signal
  loss, preventing delayed activation when the camera resumes.

## Distribution and maintenance

- Direct-install APKs are split by device ABI: arm64-v8a, armeabi-v7a, x86_64, and
  x86. Google Play continues to use the signed multi-ABI AAB.
- Optical logic, camera integration, and the AAC engine were separated into smaller
  modules with additional architecture and regression checks.
- Release optical tests now require checksum-verified public media, keep the face
  presenter visible throughout a session, and exercise meaningful AAC use before
  detector stress sequences.

## Safety scope

- This remains an early test release. It must not be the user's only or emergency
  communication method.
- Helpers should calibrate and verify the chosen access method with each user and
  device before routine use.
