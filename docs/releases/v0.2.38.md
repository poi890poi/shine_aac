# SHINE AAC v0.2.38

Android version code: 41

## Google Play Internal testing build

This release includes a signed Android App Bundle for Google Play Internal testing.

Upload `shine-aac-v0.2.38-code41-release.aab` to Play Console. The AAB is not directly installable; use Google Play Internal testing to distribute it.

## Improved

- Fundamental Zhuyin continuation symbols now precede speculative glyph and word candidates while a syllable still needs phonetic completion.
- Completed syllables retain exact-candidate priority when no phonetic continuation is needed.
- Auto demo can select required Zhuyin symbols and final candidates through bounded `更多` pages instead of timing out when a symbol moves later.
- Demo target timeouts now scale with legal scan settings, including slower scanning and reduced column counts.
- Intentional tap-to-exit ends demo mode cleanly without leaking a false error into later app behavior.

## Verification

- Full core: 212 tests passed.
- Communication benchmark: 103 tasks passed; the frozen paired baseline was not changed.
- Switch activations improved from 846 to 836, estimated scan time improved from 2,866.3 to 2,663.4 seconds, and `更多` selections improved from 68 to 63 with no task regression.
- Source browser E2E: 31 steps passed.
- Packaged WebView E2E: 32 steps passed, including the asset build and a two-column later-continuation demo commit.
