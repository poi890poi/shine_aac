# SHINE AAC v0.5.0 — release candidate

Android version code: 63. Publication is pending the release gates recorded in
[the candidate review](../reports/pre-release/v0.5.0.md). This is not a published
release announcement.

## Tablet communication

- The tablet uses the available window for the board, draft and recent messages.
  Helper controls and Settings adapt to portrait and landscape tablet windows.
- Replay status retains two readable lines. The garden remains an Easter egg,
  reached by seven taps on the software version, with a remaining-tap countdown.
- Filtered Zhuyin scan blocks avoid unnecessary gaps while retaining the approved
  first-pass behavior. Speech-voice choices match SHINE's supported languages.

## Bird garden

- Sixteen Taiwan bird species, varied licensed mountain backdrops, layered clouds,
  bushes and grassy landings share one aligned native pixel frame.
- The text-free arcade HUD puts ammo, detection status, speed and helper buttons
  on one row. Start/Replay and the POC bird picker use matching artwork with large
  accessible touch targets. SHINE selects birds randomly between rounds.
- Three rechargeable drops are the default. The alternative one-drop-per-flyby
  mode remains available to helpers between rounds.
- Training begins slowly. A successful landing advances the next round by one
  of three speed levels; a flower collision slows the current round. AAC scan
  timing informs flight speed, and the first flower has extra approach space.
- Collisions scatter feathers and give another approach; landing comes smoothly
  from the side with flight animation until touchdown.

## Distribution

Direct-install APKs use the existing pinned direct-install certificate. Play
updates use the signed AAB and Google Play's app-signing identity. A direct APK
cannot replace a Play-signed installation with a different certificate; do not
uninstall an existing communication app to bypass that distinction.

No sustained-performance or cross-user optical-accuracy improvement is claimed
by this release candidate. Detection continues to use the CPU default.
