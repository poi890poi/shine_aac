# SayToMe AAC / 我想說 0.4.1 hotfix release report

Date: 2026-08-28

Version: 0.4.1 (61)

Audited application source: `1a32628` (`codex/release-v0.4.1-hotfix`)

## Decision

- ABI-specific direct-install APKs and the signed Google Play AAB: **GO** under the
  explicitly accepted physical-device gate exception below.
- Automated source, packaged-WebView, Android unit, build, signing, and data-policy
  gates: **PASS**.
- Physical phone regression: **NOT RUN** for this hotfix at the user's request. This
  candidate must not be described as device-tested.
- Broad production rollout remains conditional on real AAC-user/helper evaluation and
  representative physical-device coverage.

The post-tag audit retained only intentional 0.4.1 changes: scan-highlight ownership
fixes, neutral phrase/Zhuyin candidate styling, stable filtered-Zhuyin grouping,
persistent after-read controls, requested defaults and wording, 3–8-column full
boards, and their tests/documentation. Camera experiments, diagnostic recorders,
private stimuli, and unrelated work are excluded.

## Remaining scope

The automated results establish deterministic logic, styling-state ownership,
packaged-asset consistency, compilation, and artifact integrity. They do not establish
behavior on a physical phone for this rebuild, comfort, fatigue, or suitability for a
particular AAC user. Maintain a second communication method during evaluation.
