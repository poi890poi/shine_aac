# SayToMe AAC / 我想說 Play Store Release Notes

Current Play Console "what's new" text for v0.4.0 (code 60), in the bilingual
(`zh-TW` primary, `en-IN` fallback) format Google Play requires. Replace this whole block
on the next release; do not keep a per-version history here.

Google caps release notes at 500 Unicode characters per language (Play Console Help,
[Prepare and roll out a release](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859348)).
The blocks below are within that limit.

<zh-TW>

早期測試版本，主要給台灣繁體中文使用者與協助者試用。

- 新增 USB UVC 外接相機，可用長眨眼或臉頰抽動控制掃描；影像只在裝置上處理。
- 新增原生協助者設定與獨立進階時間設定頁，保留既有設定資料。
- 改善相機選擇、權限、重新連接、預覽、個人化校正與偵測可靠度。
- 可選擇「持續連選」，以一次維持動作依序選區塊、第一列及第一格；選到一格後必須放鬆。
- 可設定只在掃描停止後進入待機：降低螢幕亮度與相機分析負載，下一次相機動作只恢復掃描。
- 新增 USB 相機、相機動作、進階設定與語音下載專頁。
- 仍是早期開發版本，不能作為唯一或緊急溝通方式。

</zh-TW>
<en-IN>

Early test release.

- Adds direct USB UVC cameras for calibrated blink/twitch scanning; frames stay on device.
- Adds native helper Settings and dedicated advanced guides.
- Optional Hold to advance crosses the hierarchy once, then requires release.
- Optional Idle timeout dims and reduces camera work only after scanning stops; the next gesture only resumes.
- Improves camera setup, calibration, reconnection, and reliability.
- Not for sole or emergency communication.

</en-IN>
