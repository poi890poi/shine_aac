# SHINE AAC v0.2.42

Android version code: 45

## Taiwan speech

- Added a standard Android-style Taiwan voice list with immediate selection,
  inline previews, downloaded/online status, gender labels when the engine
  exposes enough metadata, and a shortcut to Android's speech-data manager.
- Added one lightweight built-in option, `教育部人聲注音`, and made it the
  default. It uses Ministry of Education recordings for Bopomofo symbols and
  the selected Android voice for other text.
- Kept explicitly selected Android voices available and unchanged.
- Registered the standalone AAC prompt `重選` through Android's standard
  `TextToSpeech.addSpeech()` API so it reads `ㄔㄨㄥˊ ㄒㄩㄢˇ` without changing
  or lengthening the visible label.
- Kept the package light: no experimental full voice model, phrase library, or
  per-button recordings are included. The only custom general-text audio is
  the 591 ms `重選` correction.

## Compatibility

- Voice names and availability depend on the Android device and its active text-to-speech engine.
- Downloaded voice packages remain managed by Android and the selected engine;
  the app does not bundle or redistribute third-party voice packages.
